# G01.SG04.PR02.ST02.T01.C01.I01

## Role
Implement and prove automatic requested-city verification plus bounded session refresh/rebootstrap. Stay within the accepted PR01 contract.

## Goal
Ensure an OzonContext cannot be used for a successful regional price unless its effective context is accepted for the requested city, and ensure repeated runs need no human browser action.

## Inputs
- accepted OzonContext from PR02.ST01;
- accepted PR01 city-verification rule;
- SG02 transport.

## Actions
1. Evaluate the exact evidenced region/context signal against requested `city`.
2. Mark context verified, mismatched or unproven; never coerce unproven to success.
3. Implement bounded automatic refresh/rebootstrap for expired/rejected automatically created state.
4. Ensure refresh/retry remains on the same CityRecord ProxyContext and cannot silently migrate to direct networking or another city.
5. Expose typed status for caller/collector.

## Output Contract
Verified city-bound OzonContext or typed `context_mismatch`, `context_unproven`, transport/session/bootstrap failure.

## Acceptance
- wrong/unverified city cannot yield price success;
- repeated execution can recreate/refresh state with no profile/browser/manual city/PVZ action;
- bounded retries and deterministic failure;
- no raw credentials/session secrets in diagnostics.

## Failure Boundary
Do not weaken PR01 evidence criteria; no price parsing, collector persistence, scheduler or legacy fallback changes.
