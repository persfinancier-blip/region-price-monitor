# G01.SG04.PR04.ST01.T01.C01.I01

## Role
Integrate accepted SG04 OzonContext and outcome boundaries into the normal collector only. Preserve legacy browser/profile code for SG05.

## Goal
Make every requested `Ozon SKU × city` produce one typed proxy-first outcome without requiring `ozon_profile_dir` or manually warmed cookies in the primary path.

## Inputs
- SG01 Ozon ProductSet subset + CitySet;
- SG02 ProxyContext transport;
- accepted SG04 PR02 verified OzonContext;
- accepted SG04 PR03 parser/outcome classifier.

## Actions
1. For each city, construct/use the city ProxyContext and automatic OzonContext.
2. For each requested Ozon SKU, fetch and classify through the accepted verified context.
3. Preserve each requested SKU/city on success or failure.
4. Do not call `load_cookies`, `warm_region` or `browser_fetch_prices` as a prerequisite/automatic primary-path repair.
5. Keep legacy functions intact for later SG05 explicit fallback ownership.

## Output Contract
One typed Ozon outcome for every requested Ozon SKU × city, with regional price on success.

## Acceptance
- no primary profile/cookies prerequisite;
- all network calls use city ProxyContext;
- only verified city context can yield success;
- one failed unit does not erase siblings;
- no browser launch in normal path.

## Failure Boundary
Do not redesign legacy fallback, DB/CSV persistence, scheduling or WB behavior.
