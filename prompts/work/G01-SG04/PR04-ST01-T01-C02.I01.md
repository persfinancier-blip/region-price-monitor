# G01.SG04.PR04.ST01.T01.C02.I01

## Role
Integrate the repaired C02 autonomous OzonContext and typed outcome boundaries into the primary collector only. Preserve SG05 manual legacy fallback separately.

## Goal
Make every requested `Ozon SKU × city` produce one typed proxy-first autonomous outcome without manually warmed cookies/profile, while permitting hidden/headless Selenium/Chrome as an internal automatic engine when the accepted C02 strategy requires it.

## Inputs
- SG01 Ozon ProductSet subset + CitySet;
- SG02 ProxyContext authority;
- accepted SG04 PR02 C02 verified OzonContext engine;
- accepted SG04 PR03 C02 parser/outcome classifier.

## Actions
1. For each city, construct/use the city ProxyContext and automatic OzonContext.
2. Allow the accepted OzonContext engine to use `curl_cffi`, hidden/headless Selenium/Chrome, or their evidenced bounded combination.
3. For each requested Ozon SKU, fetch/classify only through verified requested-city context.
4. Preserve each requested SKU/city on success or failure.
5. Do not call legacy `load_cookies`, `warm_region` or `browser_fetch_prices` as primary prerequisites or automatic manual repair.
6. Do not forbid a **new non-interactive hidden browser engine** merely because SG05 preserves the old visible/manual browser capability.
7. A challenge requiring human action remains explicit failure in primary; do not automatically cross into SG05.

## Output Contract
One typed Ozon outcome for every requested Ozon SKU × city, with regional price on success and safe engine provenance.

## Acceptance
- no primary manually prepared profile/cookies prerequisite;
- all HTTP and hidden-browser network activity derives from city ProxyContext;
- hidden browser is zero-interaction and bounded;
- only verified city context can yield success;
- one failed unit does not erase siblings;
- no implicit visible/manual browser fallback.

## Failure Boundary
Do not redesign SG05 explicit fallback, DB/CSV persistence, scheduling or WB behavior.
