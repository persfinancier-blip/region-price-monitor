4. Actual disagreement between observed stack egress locations remains fail-closed.
5. Incomplete/unparseable egress identity remains explicit `PROXY_IDENTITY_UNPROVEN`, with local ignored bodies available for diagnosis rather than guessing.
6. No direct fallback, second proxy authority, credential logging, marketplace semantic widening or hidden browser implementation.
